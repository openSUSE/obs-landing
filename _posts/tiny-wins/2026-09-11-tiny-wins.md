## 👩‍💻 Operations this week
*Statistics, deployments and incidents of [https://build.opensuse.org/](https://build.opensuse.org/)*

In the last 7 days build.opensuse.org served **22.8** million HTTP requests resulting in **2.38** million package builds.

All [trends](https://obs-measure.opensuse.org/d/bG1tPpM7z/trends) are moving sideways.

We ran **2** [deployments](https://github.com/openSUSE/open-build-service/deployments/activity_log?environment=production) of build.opensuse.org.

## 🧨 Incidents / Service Degradation

From 2026-09-08 to 2026-09-10 it was not possible to run any deployment of build.opensuse.org because a breaking change to Python macros caused cascading build failures across Python-dependent services, including `obs-server`.

All [Service Level Indicators](https://obs-measure.opensuse.org/d/-bpP1KOGk/overview?orgId=1&from=N&to=N) are green.
