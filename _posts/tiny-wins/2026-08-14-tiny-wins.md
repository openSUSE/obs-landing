## 👩‍💻 Operations this week
*Statistics, deployments and incidents of [https://build.opensuse.org/](https://build.opensuse.org/)*

In the last 7 days build.opensuse.org served **23.7** million HTTP requests resulting in **1.81** million package builds. Sign ups, builds and HTTP requests have gone down to normal levels now.

All other [trends](https://obs-measure.opensuse.org/d/bG1tPpM7z/trends) are moving sideways.

We ran **3** [deployments](https://github.com/openSUSE/open-build-service/deployments/activity_log?environment=production) of build.opensuse.org.
