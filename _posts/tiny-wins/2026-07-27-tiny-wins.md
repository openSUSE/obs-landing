## 👩‍💻 Operations this week
*Statistics, deployments and incidents of https://build.opensuse.org/*

In the last 7 days build.opensuse.org served 25.3 million HTTP requests resulting in 2.45 million package builds.
With ~200.000 builds more than last week we had again the busiest week EVER in terms of builds!

Again driven by the same namespaces...

<img src="/images/posts/2026-07-27-tiny-wins-buildstats.png" alt="Edit KIWI page" title="Edit KIWI page" style="margin: 10px;">

All other [trends](https://obs-measure.opensuse.org/d/bG1tPpM7z/trends) are moving sideways.

We ran **2** [deployments](https://github.com/openSUSE/open-build-service/deployments/activity_log?environment=production) of build.opensuse.org.

## 🧨 Incidents / Service Degradation

Each night at 01:00 UTC we are getting DoS'ed by a bunch of openSUSE internal bot's running on a schedule. We are trying to coordinate better spread
for those to avoid having to rate-limit accounts...

All [Service Level Indicators](https://obs-measure.opensuse.org/d/-bpP1KOGk/overview?orgId=1&from=N&to=N) are green, except the UI performance SLI which
is dragged down 2.5% by the DoS described above.
