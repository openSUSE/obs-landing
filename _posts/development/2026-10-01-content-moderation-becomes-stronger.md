Today we are presenting three new features that make content moderation more powerful.

*Content Moderation* is part of the [beta program](/2018/10/04/the-beta-program/).

{% include partials/_series-of-posts-about-content-moderation.md %}

## Appeals on the Report View

Previously you knew the decision about a report, but didn't know its final resolution. You had to navigate away from the page to find out what happened to it. Now that the appeals show up in the report page, you know what happened to that report from start to finish.

  <figcaption>Showing appeals on the report view</figcaption>
</figure>

## Allow Making More Than One Report Against a Reportable

The one report per person limit is now gone. If the situation changed since you reported something, you can write another report that reflects that new situation. That will give moderators an updated picture.

  <img src="/images/posts/2026-10-01-content-moderation-becomes-stronger/first-report.png" alt="First report" />

## Moderators Can Now Delete Offending Projects or Packages Directly

When content in projects or packages violates our policy but the content owner still won't act, moderators can now directly delete that project or package as part of the decision. And this will be final, as there is no appeal.

  <img src="/images/posts/2026-10-01-content-moderation-becomes-stronger/delete-project-package-decision.png" alt="Delete project decision" />
  <figcaption>Delete package decision</figcaption>
</figure>
